Careful work in a gated community

Tanoan is two gated neighborhoods, Tanoan West and Tanoan East, set around a golf course in Albuquerque’s far Northeast Heights. Each has its own entrance and its own HOA. Most projects here improve an existing property: regrading a yard that drains toward the house, tearing out tired landscaping, removing a tree that has outgrown its spot, or preparing a lot for a custom home or addition.

Working in an established neighborhood means protecting what’s already there. We plan the route in and out, protect driveways and curbs, keep the site tidy at the end of each day, and haul everything away instead of leaving a pile on the street.

Ground this close to the foothills is often decomposed granite with rock below it, and many lots slope enough that drainage matters. Grading has to move water away from your home without sending it onto a neighbor’s lot or the golf course.

Tanoan questions

Do you handle the HOA approval?

The application comes from you as the owner, but we give you a written scope of work and any project details your HOA asks for that we can provide.

Can you work without damaging my landscaping?

Yes. We plan the access route, protect hardscape and planting you want to keep, and use equipment sized for the space.

Can you fix drainage on a sloped lot?

Usually. Regrading, swales and rock-lined channels can move water away from the house. Larger or more complex sites may need an engineer’s plan.
