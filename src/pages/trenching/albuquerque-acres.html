Keeping the trench line from becoming a scar

Owners here often keep as much native vegetation as they can. A trench up the middle of the lot cuts a straight bare stripe through it, so we look at routing lines along the driveway, where ground is already disturbed.

Backfill on a slope also has to be compacted well. Loose fill in a trench that runs downhill toward the house can settle and channel runoff, which is the opposite of what the rest of the grading is trying to do.

If your subdivision has an HOA with architectural guidelines, check whether they cover restoring disturbed ground.

Albuquerque Acres trenching questions

Can the utility trench run under my driveway?

Often that's a good route, since the ground is already disturbed. It should be dug and backfilled before the driveway base goes down, so plan the order with your builder.

Does rock make trenching a lot slower here?

It can. Decomposed granite moves quickly, while caliche or boulders need breaking. During the estimate we look for rock at the surface and in any nearby cuts to judge it.

Do you dig for my well or septic system?

We dig trenches for the lines your installers specify. Septic tanks and leach fields are designed and installed by a licensed septic installer, and we can do the excavation they lay out.
