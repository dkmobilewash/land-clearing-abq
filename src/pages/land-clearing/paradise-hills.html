Complaints, the commission and the city

In the unincorporated part of the neighborhood, zoning is handled by the Paradise Hills Special Zoning District's commission rather than Bernalillo County. The commission responds to written complaints from neighbors, and overgrown weeds and trash are among the most common.

If your property is inside Albuquerque city limits instead, City of Albuquerque rules and notices apply. Either way the fix looks the same: clear the lot, haul the debris and show that it is done. We can send before-and-after photos for that.

Sand is the other half of the job. Up on the West Mesa, wind moves bare ground quickly, and a freshly cleared yard can end up drifted against the back wall. We talk through what goes back on the ground before we start, not after.

Planning to build after clearing?

Per Bernalillo County, new construction in the special zoning district needs a plot survey approved by the commission before you apply to the county for a building permit. If clearing is the first step toward an addition or a new home, start that conversation early so the clearing lines up with the approved plan.

Can your equipment get into a walled backyard?

Often. We measure the gate on the walk-through and bring a compact machine if one fits. If nothing will fit, we tell you before quoting.
