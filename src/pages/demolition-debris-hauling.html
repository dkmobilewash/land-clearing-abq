Permits and utilities come first

Demolishing a structure can require a permit, and utilities to it must be disconnected before work starts. Rules depend on the structure and the jurisdiction, so we help you confirm what is needed with the city or county before we begin.

Older buildings may contain materials such as asbestos that require testing and special handling. If we see signs of that, we will stop and tell you before anything is disturbed.
