Silt and loam that reward careful grading

Irrigated valley soil in Los Ranchos is silt and loam that grows good pasture and holds water well. That's a strength in a field and a problem near a foundation, where it keeps the ground wet long after a storm passes.

Small differences in elevation matter on ground this flat. A field off by a few inches can leave one end dry and the other underwater, and a yard that sits level with the house sends water toward it.

Working around ditches, trees and open space

Most irrigation here runs through acequias usually managed by the Middle Rio Grande Conservancy District. We grade up to the ditch easement and leave the ditch, its banks and its gates alone unless the district has approved the work.

The village places real value on open space and mature trees. Before grading near a big cottonwood or an old tree row, ask the village whether tree rules apply, and we'll lay out the work to keep heavy equipment off the roots.

Drainage & erosion questions in Los Ranchos de Albuquerque

Can you regrade a pasture without ruining the irrigation flow?

Yes, that's the point of doing it. We find where water enters the field and grade a consistent fall away from the turnout so it reaches the far end instead of pooling near the gate.

Should storm runoff go into the acequia?

Not without permission. Sending water into a ditch or changing its banks needs approval from whoever manages it, usually MRGCD. We keep storm water on your property or route it to an allowed outlet.

How do I keep my barn aisle dry in summer storms?

Usually by raising the grade at the entrance, carrying roof water away with a swale or rock rundown, and making sure the ground falls away from the doors.

Check with the village on larger jobs

Los Ranchos is its own municipality with its own zoning and permits, separate from Albuquerque and Bernalillo County. Substantial regrading or work tied to a new structure may need village review. We request a New Mexico 811 locate before any digging and help you work out which questions to bring to the village.
