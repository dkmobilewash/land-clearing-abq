Sloped lots above a golf course

Many homes here sit on lots that fall noticeably from front to back or side to side. Decomposed granite sheds water quickly, and in a monsoon downpour the runoff gains speed and washes out mulch beds, gravel paths and the base of walls.

Whatever we build has to carry water away from your house without pushing it onto the next lot or the golf course. That usually means slowing it with rock, spreading it with swales, and keeping it on your property where it can soak in.

Drainage & erosion questions in Tanoan

Can water from my yard drain onto the golf course?

It shouldn't be redirected there. Grading has to avoid pushing runoff onto neighbors or the course, so we design swales and rock areas that move water safely across your lot and off it only where that's allowed.

Will drainage work tear up my existing landscaping?

Some disturbance is unavoidable where the grade changes, but we limit it to the work area and protect the plants and hardscape you want to keep. We agree on that boundary before starting.

Why does decomposed granite wash away so easily?

It's made of small, loose grains with little binding them together. On a slope, fast-moving water picks it up readily, which is why rock rundowns and a slower flow path matter on these lots.
