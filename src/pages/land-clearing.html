Clearing land in the high desert

Albuquerque lots tend to hide surprises under the brush: buried debris, old foundations, rock, and in many areas a hard caliche layer just below the surface. We look for these during the estimate so your quote reflects what is really there.

Clearing larger areas can kick up a lot of dust, and in Albuquerque and Bernalillo County some projects need a fugitive dust control permit from the local air quality program. Requirements depend on the size and type of project, so we help you check what applies before work begins.
