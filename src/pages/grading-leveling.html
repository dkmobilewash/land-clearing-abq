Albuquerque know-how

Grading and drainage rules

Grading must not push stormwater onto your neighbors. In Albuquerque and Bernalillo County, larger or more complex projects may need an approved grading and drainage plan, and lots next to arroyos may have extra requirements from the city, county or flood control authority.

We will tell you if we think your project may need a plan or permit, and we are happy to work from plans prepared by your engineer. We do not make promises about permit outcomes — those decisions belong to the agency.

FAQ

Grading & Leveling questions

What is the difference between rough and finish grading?

Rough grading sets the overall shape and elevations. Finish grading is the final, smoother pass that prepares the surface for landscaping, gravel or concrete.

Can you fix a yard that floods?

Often, yes. Re-grading to move water away from the house is usually the first step, sometimes combined with swales or other drainage work.
