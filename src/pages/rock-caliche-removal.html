What to expect

Caliche is a natural layer of soil cemented together by calcium carbonate. It is common in the Southwest and can be so hard that a shovel just bounces off it. It also blocks drainage and roots, which is why plants and trees often struggle above it.

We use excavators with rippers and hydraulic hammers to break caliche and rock, then haul it away or reuse it on site.

Common questions

How do I know if I have caliche?

If you hit a hard, whitish, chalky layer that will not dig, it is likely caliche. We can confirm it during the estimate.

Does caliche affect drainage?

Yes. Water often cannot soak through it, which can cause pooling and poor plant growth. Breaking through it can help.
