Rocky ground changes the stump plan

The land rises toward the Sandia foothills, and the soil gets rockier as it climbs. Decomposed granite, buried rock and caliche are common, and roots grow around and between them.

Grinding a stump in that ground is slower, and pulling one can bring up rock with the root ball. When a stump sits inside a future pad, pulling it is worth the extra effort, since the pad has to be compacted on solid material.

Some lots have an arroyo or an area mapped in a flood zone. Vegetation along an arroyo may help hold that ground in place, so we ask about any approvals before clearing along the channel.

Tree removal as part of custom-home site prep

  1. 1

    Review the plans and staking

    We work from your builder's or engineer's site plan, so we know which trees fall in the pad, driveway and utility routes.

  2. 2

    Mark what stays

    Native plants and trees outside the work area are flagged so they come through the job intact.

  3. 3

    Pull stumps in the footprint

    Inside the building area, stumps and main roots come out completely. We request a New Mexico 811 locate before digging them.

  4. 4

    Grind stumps outside it

    Where the ground will be landscaped rather than built on, grinding below grade is usually enough.

  5. 5

    Backfill and hand off

    Holes are backfilled and compacted in lifts so the pad and driveway work can start.

Tree & stump removal questions in Albuquerque Acres

Is my lot under city or county rules for tree removal?

It depends on the parcel. Some of North Albuquerque Acres is inside the City of Albuquerque and some is in Bernalillo County. Check yours, along with any subdivision HOA guidelines, before we schedule.

Can you take out a few trees to open up my mountain view?

Yes. We remove only the trees you choose and leave the rest. Show us the view you want during the walk-through and we'll talk through which trees are blocking it.

Will the stump holes in my pad area be compacted?

Yes. We backfill and compact in lifts so your builder isn't pouring a foundation over soft spots.
