Protecting the landscaping around the tree

Tanoan lots are landscaped, and much of that work sits right where a crew has to walk or roll a machine. We lay out the route ahead of time, use equipment sized for side yards and gates, and protect driveways and curbs.

Decomposed granite and buried rock are common this close to the foothills. A stump wrapped around rock takes more grinding, and pulling one on a sloped lot can disturb the grade, so grinding is usually the right call unless something will be built in that spot.

What affects a Tanoan tree job

Access and protection, more than tree size, drive most of the effort here.

Distance from the street
A backyard tree means carrying wood and chips out through a side yard or gate.
Nearby structures
Trees close to the roof, a wall or a pool need more sectional cuts and more lowering by rope.
Slope
Sloped lots limit where machines can sit and how a stump hole can be filled without changing drainage.
Rock in the root zone
Buried rock in decomposed granite slows the grinder down.
HOA working hours
Limited hours can stretch a job across more days.

Tanoan tree removal questions

Do I need HOA approval just to remove one tree?

Many HOAs treat tree removal as an exterior change, but the answer is in your own HOA's rules. Check with the architectural committee before we schedule.

Can you remove a tree on my lot that backs onto the golf course?

Yes, if it's on your property, with care to keep debris and equipment off the course. Trees on HOA or course property are a question for the HOA.

Can you take out a stump buried under rock mulch?

Yes. We pull back the rock around the stump, grind it out, and can rake the rock back over the spot afterward.

Don't let a stump hole send water toward the house

On a sloped Tanoan lot, filling and finishing a stump hole is a small grading job. We match the surrounding grade so runoff keeps moving away from your home and doesn't head toward a neighbor's lot or the golf course.
