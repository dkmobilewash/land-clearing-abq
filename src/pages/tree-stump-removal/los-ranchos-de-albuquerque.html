Timing the work around irrigation

Irrigated valley silt and loam is soft right after water comes through. If a field has just been flooded, we hold off on driving an excavator across it until it firms up.

Telling us your irrigation schedule up front lets us pick a day when the ground can carry equipment without leaving ruts in your field.

Grinding is the lighter option on soft ground. When a stump has to be pulled, for a new fence or a regraded paddock, we schedule that part for the driest window.

Trees in power lines stay with the utility

Ditch banks and fence lines often run under overhead lines. If a tree is near those lines, contact your electric utility first. Qualified line-clearance crews handle that work, and we don't cut in that zone.

Tree & stump removal questions in Los Ranchos de Albuquerque

Can you clear olive trees growing inside my ditch?

Not without approval. Ditches and their easements are usually managed by MRGCD, and work in or on them needs the district's permission. Once that's in hand, we can talk through how to do it.

Do you haul away a whole tree row?

Yes. Limbs are chipped, trunks are hauled off and stump holes are backfilled. If you'd rather keep firewood or chips, we leave them where you want them.

Should fence-line stumps be ground or pulled?

Grinding takes the stump below the surface and leaves the roots, which is fine if the fence stays where it is. Pulling removes the root ball, which is better if you're setting new posts in the same line.
