What tree removal looks like on the mesa

A dead windbreak along the back wall

Rows planted to block the wind often decline after a few dry years. We take out the dead ones, grind their stumps and leave the healthy trees standing.

A lot you've owned for years

Parcels in Rio Rancho Estates and other older sections sometimes have a few volunteer trees growing among the chamisa. We can walk the lot without you, send photos and go over the removal by phone.

Stumps in a future building footprint

Before a pad is built, stumps and main roots need to come out. We pull them and backfill so the grading work starts on clean ground.

What's left after a windstorm

Wind across the mesa can bring down trees that were already weak. If you've cleaned up the trunk yourself, we can grind out what remains.

Sand, wind and a stump hole

Sandy soil is easy on a stump grinder, but it doesn't hold its shape. A pulled stump leaves a loose hole that has to be backfilled and compacted, or it settles later, which matters most under a driveway or slab.

Bare sand also blows. When we remove a row of trees that has been sheltering a yard, we talk with you about what will hold the ground afterward, such as leaving wood chips, laying gravel or planting replacements of your own.

Some lots hit caliche a short way down, and roots tend to spread sideways above it. We check for it during the estimate whenever a stump needs to be pulled.

What shapes the work on a Rio Rancho lot

No two quotes look alike here. These are the things we look at during the walk-through.

Road access
Lots on unpaved roads at the city's edges need a route a truck and trailer can use without sinking into sand.
How many stumps
One stump in a front yard is a small job. A full row of old windbreak trees is a much bigger one.
Grind or pull
Pulling takes more work but leaves the ground ready for construction.
Where the wood goes
Haul-off is included unless you want chips or firewood left behind.
Lot boundaries
Survey pins on older lots can be buried or missing. If a tree sits near a line, have a surveyor mark the corners before we take it out.

Tree & stump removal questions in Rio Rancho

I live out of state. Can you handle tree removal on my Rio Rancho lot?

Yes. We walk the lot on our own, send photos, review the scope with you by phone and put it in a written quote before anything is scheduled.

Will grinding a stump in sandy soil leave a pit?

Grinding leaves a shallow area filled with chips and soil, and we rake it level. If you want the spot ready for gravel or landscaping, we can haul the grindings off and fill it with soil instead.

Should stumps come out before my builder grades the pad?

Yes, if they're inside the footprint or driveway. Roots left under a pad can rot and leave voids, so we pull them, backfill and compact the holes in lifts.
