How a Tanoan regrade usually goes

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Protect what stays

    Before machines go in, we plan the route from the gate and cover the driveway, curbs and planting we aren't touching.

  4. 4

    Locate, cut and fill

    We request a New Mexico 811 locate, then rework the grade. Rock buried in the decomposed granite is pulled out or broken up.

  5. 5

    Finish grade and daily cleanup

    The last pass sets the slope away from the house. We tidy the site at the end of every day and haul everything off.

Grading & leveling questions in Tanoan

Can you stick to the HOA's contractor hours?

Yes. Send us your HOA's rules on working hours and contractor vehicles, and we'll plan each day's work inside them.

How do you get equipment through the gate?

We set up access with you and the HOA ahead of time so the crew and trailer are expected. Mention it early if your HOA has a specific process.

Will regrading my yard push water onto the golf course?

It shouldn't. We grade so runoff goes where it's allowed. If the only outlet is toward the course or a neighbor, we'll talk through other options, like a swale, before any work starts.
