Built on the mesa, one lot at a time

Much of Rio Rancho was divided into lots long before roads and utilities reached them, which is why so many parcels, especially out in Rio Rancho Estates, are still raw desert today. Owners often buy a lot, hold it for years, then need it cleared all at once to sell it or build on it.

The mesa is windy and the soil is sandy. Once the brush comes off, bare ground starts to blow, so we plan clearing so the site isn’t left bare any longer than it has to be, and we use water or other dust control when the job calls for it.

On the unpaved roads at the edges of the city, access is part of the job. We look at how a truck and trailer will reach the lot, and whether a driveway or turnaround should be cut before construction traffic starts.

Before clearing a lot in Rio Rancho

  • City of Rio Rancho rules. Rio Rancho has its own building and development requirements, separate from Albuquerque’s. Check with the city before a build; what you need depends on your lot and project.
  • Weeds and litter. The city can require owners to keep lots clear of weeds and litter. If you’ve received a notice, call us with the deadline.
  • Find your corners. On older vacant lots, survey pins can be buried or missing. If you aren’t sure where your boundaries are, have a surveyor mark them before we clear.
  • Utilities. We request a New Mexico 811 locate before any trenching or digging.

Rules change and vary by property. We help you check what applies; we don’t give legal advice or guarantee permit decisions.

Rio Rancho questions

Can you clear a lot I bought years ago and have never seen?

Yes. Send us the address or parcel number. We can walk the lot without you and go over it with you by phone, with photos.

Do you build pads for new homes in Rio Rancho?

Yes. We clear, grade and compact building pads, working from your builder’s or engineer’s plans and staking.

Is Rio Rancho soil hard to build on?

Much of it is sandy and easy to move, but it blows and erodes. Some lots also have caliche below the surface. We check during the estimate.
