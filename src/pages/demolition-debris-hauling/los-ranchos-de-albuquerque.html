Clearing outbuildings without losing the rural feel

The Village of Los Ranchos de Albuquerque values open space and mature trees, and most owners want a demolition to leave the property feeling more open, not scraped. We plan the removal around the trees and fields you're keeping.

Many outbuildings here were built under the trees that shade them or beside the ditch that waters the field. That means slower, more careful work, with debris staged well away from the acequia.

Irrigated silt and loam soften after water comes through. We haul when the ground can carry loaded trucks so the field isn't left rutted.

What changes the scope of a Los Ranchos demolition

These are the details that make one job here bigger than another.

Structure size and materials
A wood shed is simpler than a barn with metal roofing and a concrete floor.
Distance from the drive
An outbuilding at the back of a field means longer hauls across the property.
Mature trees nearby
Shade trees beside a structure call for piece-by-piece work and protection for their roots.
Concrete and footings
Slabs, footings and old foundations add breaking and hauling.
Hazardous materials
Paint, chemicals, tires and batteries need special disposal and are planned for separately.

Village permits and utility shutoffs

Los Ranchos has its own zoning and permits, separate from Albuquerque. Removing a structure can require a village permit, so ask the village before we put you on the schedule.

Power, water and gas to the structure must be disconnected before demolition. Older outbuildings may contain materials such as asbestos that need testing, and if we see signs of it, we stop and tell you.

Questions to ask the village

  • Does removing this structure need a permit?
  • Do any rules cover the trees near it?
  • Is anything required before I build on the cleared spot?

Demolition & hauling questions in Los Ranchos de Albuquerque

Can you remove a structure that sits on the ditch easement?

Work in or on a ditch or its easement usually needs MRGCD approval first. Once that's settled, we plan the removal so nothing ends up in the water.

There's a big cottonwood right next to the shed. Can it stay?

Yes. We pull the structure down toward the open side, keep machines off the root zone where we can, and keep swinging debris away from the trunk.

Can you leave the broken concrete for me to use?

Haul-off is included by default. If you want broken concrete left for a particular use, tell us at the estimate and we'll talk through whether it makes sense for that spot.
