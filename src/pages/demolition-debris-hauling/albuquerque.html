Albuquerque demolition jobs, start to finish

Making way for a garage or casita

An old shed, slab or block wall is often sitting right where a new garage or casita will go. We take it out, break up the footings and leave the spot ready for grading.

Dumping on a vacant lot

Vacant lots in the city attract mattresses, furniture, construction scraps and household trash. We clear it, haul it away and can suggest ways to discourage the next round.

Answering a weed and litter notice

If code enforcement has flagged the property, we clear the debris along with the weeds and send before-and-after photos you can share with the city.

An old driveway or patio

Cracked concrete is broken out and hauled, and the base is left ready for whatever replaces it.

Permits and shutoffs come before demolition

Taking down a structure inside city limits can require a City of Albuquerque permit, and any electric, gas or water service to it must be disconnected before work starts. We help you confirm what your project needs, but the permit and the disconnects have to be in place before we begin.

How a city demolition job runs

  1. 1

    Estimate and scope

    We walk the site, note the structure's age, materials and utilities, and write up what's coming down and what's being hauled.

  2. 2

    Permits and disconnects

    You confirm any required permit with the City of Albuquerque and have service to the structure shut off and disconnected. We help you figure out what to ask.

  3. 3

    Check for suspect materials

    Older buildings may contain materials such as asbestos that need testing. If we see signs of that, we stop and tell you before anything is disturbed.

  4. 4

    Tear-down and footings

    Equipment is sized to the space, from compact machines in a walled backyard to an excavator on an open lot. We request a New Mexico 811 locate before digging out footings.

  5. 5

    Final pickup

    Nails, scraps and broken pieces are picked up so the site is left clean.

Demolition & hauling questions in Albuquerque

Can you demolish a shed in a backyard that only has alley access?

Often, yes. We check the alley width, overhead lines and walls during the estimate, then choose equipment that fits. Some debris may need to be carried out by hand.

Will you take the paint cans and old tires stored in my shed?

Tell us about them first. Paint, chemicals, tires and batteries need special disposal and can't simply go in with the rest of the debris.

My garage was built decades ago. Is that a problem for demolition?

Age alone isn't a problem, but older buildings may contain materials like asbestos that need testing first. If we see signs of it, we stop and let you know before work continues.
