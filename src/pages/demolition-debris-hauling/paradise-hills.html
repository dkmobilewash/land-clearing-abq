Taking down a backyard structure, step by step

  1. 1

    Check which rules apply

    Find out whether your address is in the special zoning district or inside Albuquerque city limits, and whether removing the structure needs a permit.

  2. 2

    Disconnect utilities

    Any power or water run to the structure has to be shut off and disconnected before demolition.

  3. 3

    Look for older materials

    Older structures may contain materials like asbestos that need testing. If we see signs of that, we stop and tell you.

  4. 4

    Demolish, sort and haul

    Wood, metal and concrete are separated where practical and hauled off.

  5. 5

    Leave the ground stable

    Sandy mesa soil blows once it's bare, so if nothing goes on the spot right away, we talk about covering it with gravel, rock or mulch.

What drives the size of a Paradise Hills job

Most jobs here are modest, but a few things make them bigger.

Gate and side-yard width
Narrow access means more carrying by hand to the trailer.
Slab thickness and footings
A thin patio breaks up more easily than a thick slab with footings under it.
How much is mixed together
Junk tangled with soil, brush and concrete takes longer to sort.
Hazardous items
Paint, chemicals, tires and batteries need special disposal and are handled on their own.

Demolition & hauling questions in Paradise Hills

Can you remove an old block wall between my yard and the neighbor's?

We can take out old walls, but a shared wall is a conversation with your neighbor first, and possibly a boundary question. Once you both agree, we break it out and haul the block.

Can you take out a shed slab without wrecking the rest of my yard?

We break out the slab with equipment sized to the yard, load from the closest access point and keep traffic off areas you want protected.

What's left after you remove an old slab?

Bare ground with the old base exposed. We pick up the concrete and debris and can grade the spot level. If something new is going there, your builder will tell you what base it needs.
