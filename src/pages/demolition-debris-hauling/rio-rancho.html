Years of sitting empty leave a mess

Rio Rancho has a lot of vacant land, and much of it, especially in Rio Rancho Estates, is far from neighbors who would notice dumping. By the time an owner comes back to sell or build, there can be furniture, tires, yard waste and construction scraps spread across the lot.

Hauling it out depends on access. On the unpaved roads at the city's edges, we check whether a loaded truck and trailer can get in and out without bogging down in sand, and plan the loads around that.

Once the debris is gone, a bare sandy lot starts to blow. If you're clearing for a build, we can line up the debris haul with the brush clearing so the ground isn't left open longer than it needs to be.

Demolition & hauling questions in Rio Rancho

Can you help me answer a weed and litter notice from the city?

Yes. Debris piles on a Rio Rancho lot can draw a city weed and litter notice just like overgrowth does. Send us the notice and its deadline, and we'll quote the debris and the weeds together.

There's an old slab on my lot. Does it have to go before I build?

That's a question for your builder and plans. If it's in the way of the new footprint, we break it out and haul it, and we request a New Mexico 811 locate before digging out footings.

Can you find my lot corners while you're out there?

No, that's a surveyor's job. On older lots the survey pins can be buried or missing, so if you're unsure of your boundaries, have them marked before we haul anything near the line.
