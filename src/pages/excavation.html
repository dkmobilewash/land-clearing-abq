Always call 811 before digging

Before any excavation, we submit a locate request through New Mexico 811 so underground utilities are marked. It is required before digging and it protects you, our crew and your neighbors. Locates take a few working days, so we build that into the schedule.

Albuquerque soils range from loose sand on the West Mesa to hard caliche and rock in the foothills. We check ground conditions during the estimate so the quote reflects the digging you will actually get.

Common questions

Do I need a permit for excavation?

Many excavation projects are part of a permitted building project. Requirements depend on what you are building and where, so check with the city or county — we can help you figure out the questions to ask.

What do you do with the dirt?

We can spread it on site, stockpile it for backfill, or haul it away. If you need fill, we can bring it in.
