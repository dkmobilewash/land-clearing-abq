Locates and inspections

We request a New Mexico 811 locate before every trench. Marked utilities are hand-exposed where required, and we dig carefully around them.

Many utility installations must be inspected before the trench is backfilled. We coordinate with you and your installer so the trench stays open until it has been inspected.

Common questions

Do you install the pipe or wire?

We dig and backfill. The line itself is typically installed by your licensed plumber, electrician or gas installer.

How deep can you trench?

Depth depends on the line and the code requirements for it. Tell us what you are installing and we will dig to the depth your installer specifies.
