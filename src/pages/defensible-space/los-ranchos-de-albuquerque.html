Open fields and dry grass in a river village

Los Ranchos sits in the North Valley near the Rio Grande bosque, and its fields are a big part of why people live here. Between irrigations, though, pasture grass and weeds dry out, and a field that runs right up to a house or barn puts fuel close to the walls.

Ditch banks and fence lines add another layer. Russian olive and Siberian elm grow thick there, and the dead wood beneath them builds up year after year.

Thinning these areas is practical upkeep. It reduces fuel; it doesn't mean a home is safe from fire, and we don't suggest otherwise.

Fuel we usually target on Los Ranchos land

  • Dry pasture grass and weeds between the field and the house
  • Russian olive and Siberian elm thickets on fence lines
  • Dead wood and litter under mature cottonwoods and orchard trees
  • Low limbs that give fire a path up into the canopy
  • Hay, pallets and debris stored against barns
  • Brush crowding the driveway and gates

Check with the village before big trees come down

The Village of Los Ranchos values its mature trees. Before removing any large tree as part of fuel work, ask the village whether rules apply to your property. For spacing and clearance recommendations, follow guidance from your local fire department, New Mexico fire and forestry agencies or a Firewise program.

Defensible space questions in Los Ranchos de Albuquerque

Should I mow my whole pasture short before fire season?

Not necessarily. Many owners keep the field and only cut a short-grass band around buildings, which keeps the land useful while lowering fuel near the house. Your fire department can advise on what's sensible for your property.

Can you thin the Russian olive along my ditch bank?

We can thin on your side up to the ditch easement. Work on the ditch bank itself usually needs approval from MRGCD.

Can you work while my horses and hay are on the property?

Yes. Horses need to be moved to a pen away from the work, and we'll clear around hay storage carefully and haul the cut material out the same day where we can.
