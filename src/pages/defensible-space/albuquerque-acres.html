What drives fuel work on an Acres lot

Lots here are big, so the question is usually where to focus, not whether to clear everything.

Slope
Land rises toward the mountains, and steeper ground below a house usually gets more attention. It also means more hand work.
Rock and boulders
Decomposed granite and rock limit where machines can go, so crews thin by hand in the rough spots.
How close brush grows to the house
A home with native shrubs right against the stucco takes more work than one with a gravel apron.
Vacant lots next door
We can only work your parcel, so an untouched neighbor lot shapes where we concentrate.
Haul-off distance
Long driveways and big lots mean more carrying and loading.

Fuel reduction you can plan into a new build

North Albuquerque Acres is one of the busiest areas around Albuquerque for custom-home site prep, and that's the easiest moment to think about fuel. While we're already clearing for the pad and driveway, thinning the surrounding brush is a small addition.

Doing it then also lets your landscaper plan the ground right around the house with fire in mind. Follow guidance from New Mexico fire and forestry agencies, your local fire department or a Firewise program for what that should look like on your slope.

Keeping the native look while cutting the fuel

Most owners here don't want a bare lot. We thin rather than clear: removing dead plants, spacing out crowded shrubs and raising low limbs, so the lot still looks like the foothills.

Thinning lowers the fuel around a home, but it doesn't guarantee a house will make it through a fire.

Defensible space questions in Albuquerque Acres

Can fuel reduction be part of my site prep?

Yes. If we're clearing your pad and driveway, we can thin the brush around the home site at the same time, so it's one mobilization instead of two.

Will my HOA let me thin native vegetation?

Some subdivisions here have architectural guidelines that cover landscaping. Check yours before work starts; we'll thin to whatever they allow.

What happens to the cut brush on a rocky slope?

We carry it to where it can be loaded and haul it off. Leaving piles on a slope just moves the fuel around.
