Village first, then the ditch

Los Ranchos has its own zoning and permits, separate from Albuquerque, so check with the village before a new structure or a large dig. Acequias and their easements are usually managed by MRGCD, and work in or on a ditch needs their approval. If the excavation will reach the roots of a large tree, ask the village whether tree rules apply first.

Building something without losing the rural lot

Many people in the village chose their property for the open fields and old trees. A dig shouldn't cost you either. We stage spoil away from tree roots, keep trucks on the driveway or a single field track, and put topsoil back where it came from.

When the work is done, we grade the disturbed strip so irrigation water still spreads the way it did before. If a pasture needs reseeding afterward, that's up to you, but the ground will be ready for it.

Excavation questions in Los Ranchos

Can the topsoil from my dig be saved for the pasture?

Yes. We strip it before the deeper excavation and stockpile it separately, then spread it back over the disturbed area or wherever you want it.

Do I need village approval to dig a pond in Los Ranchos?

Possibly. Ask the Village of Los Ranchos about the specific project, and MRGCD if the pond is near a ditch or would tie into irrigation. We'll give you a written scope to bring to them.

Can you excavate close to the acequia along my property line?

Only with MRGCD's permission for anything in or next to the ditch or its easement. Outside that area, we keep equipment and spoil well clear of the bank.
