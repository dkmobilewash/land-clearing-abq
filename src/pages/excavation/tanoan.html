Decomposed granite, with rock where you least want it

This close to the foothills, most yards are decomposed granite. It digs cleanly until the bucket finds a buried boulder, and in a pool dig that can happen more than once. We plan for breaking or lifting rock rather than treating it as a surprise.

Slope is the other factor. Many lots here fall toward a street, a neighbor or the golf course, and a retaining-wall cut or pool changes where water goes. Backfill and final shaping have to move runoff away from your house without sending it next door or onto the course.

Questions about digging behind the gate

Can you get an excavator into a Tanoan backyard?

Usually. We measure the gate and side yard during the estimate and bring a compact machine sized to fit. If a wall section or gate needs to come out temporarily, we'll talk it through first.

Where does the dirt from a pool dig go?

Most of it is hauled away, since these lots rarely have room for a pile. Some can stay for backfill around the shell if you and your pool builder want it.

Will you follow our HOA's contractor hours?

Yes. Send us the rules for your HOA and we'll plan the dig and truck traffic around them.
