Albuquerque know-how

Permits and dust on larger sites

Larger disturbed sites may need a fugitive dust control permit from the local air quality program in Albuquerque and Bernalillo County, and may need stormwater coverage under the federal NPDES Construction General Permit. Whether these apply depends on the size and nature of the project.

We check these items with you early, before work starts, and we follow any dust and erosion controls your permits require.

FAQ

Site Prep & Building Pads questions

Do you work with my builder?

Yes. We regularly coordinate with builders, engineers and surveyors and work from their plans and staking.

How is a pad compacted?

Fill is placed in layers (lifts), moisture-conditioned and compacted. If your project requires compaction testing, we coordinate with the testing company.
