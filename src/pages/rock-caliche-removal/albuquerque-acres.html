What makes rock harder to remove on some Acres lots

The land rises toward the mountains, and conditions shift from one parcel to the next.

Where the rock starts
On some lots it's near the surface; on others the decomposed granite runs deep before anything hard appears.
Solid layer or scattered boulders
Individual boulders can be lifted out. A continuous caliche or rock layer has to be broken across the whole footprint.
Basement versus slab
A basement goes much deeper and usually meets more rock.
Trench length
Water, sewer and power runs on a large lot can be long, and rock along the route adds up.
What happens to the material
Reusing rock on site saves hauling but takes time to sort and place.

Rock work during a custom-home build

  1. 1

    Test dig at the estimate

    Where we can, we dig in the footprint to see what's below before quoting.

  2. 2

    Locate first

    A New Mexico 811 request goes in before breaking starts, and we stay clear of the marks.

  3. 3

    Break within the plan

    We break only what the foundation, basement, trenches or driveway need, working from the builder's staking.

  4. 4

    Sort as we go

    Boulders worth keeping are set aside; the rest is loaded for haul-off or kept for rough fill.

Trenches through rock deserve their own line in the budget

On big lots, the utility trenches from the street to the house can run a long way, and rock in them is easy to overlook. Ask your builder whether trench rock is in their numbers. We're glad to look at the route during the same visit as the foundation area.

Boulders as part of the finished lot

Owners in Albuquerque Acres usually want the finished site to look like it belongs below the Sandias. Boulders that came out of your own ground are the most natural fit.

We can set them along the driveway, at the edge of native areas or to help steer runoff on sloped sections, following your landscape plan. Whatever you don't want leaves on our trucks.

Rock and caliche questions for Albuquerque Acres

Can you break rock in the utility trenches as well as the foundation?

Yes. We handle rock anywhere the site work needs it: footings, basement, trenches and driveway cuts. Covering them on one visit is usually simplest.

Can boulders from my lot be reused for landscaping?

Yes. We set aside the ones worth keeping during the dig and place them where you want once construction allows.

Should I check for rock before buying a lot in the Acres?

It's a reasonable step. A test dig can tell you whether rock or caliche is close to the surface, which affects site prep. We can walk a vacant lot and send photos if you're not there.
