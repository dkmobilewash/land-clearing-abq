What to expect

A good gravel driveway starts underneath: a shaped subgrade with a crown or cross-slope so water runs off, compacted base course, and the right surface gravel on top.

We build new driveways, widen and extend existing ones, and re-grade rutted rural roads and long ranch drives.

Built for monsoon season

Summer monsoon storms can drop a lot of water fast, and an unshaped driveway turns into a channel. We grade driveways so water sheds off to the sides, and add drainage dips or culverts where water needs to cross.

Where a driveway meets a public road, you may need approval from the road authority. We will flag that during the estimate.

Common questions

What gravel is best for a driveway?

It depends on use and slope. Compacted base course with a crushed surface gravel is a common choice. We will recommend options and explain the trade-offs.

Can you fix my washed-out driveway?

Yes. We re-grade, rebuild the base where needed, and add drainage features so it holds up better next time.
