What to expect

Weeds and tumbleweeds grow fast after summer monsoon rains and dry out into a fire hazard by fall. When the wind picks up, tumbleweeds pile against walls, fences and neighbors’ yards. Regular clearing keeps a property safe, presentable and out of trouble with code enforcement.

We handle everything from a single overgrown backyard to large vacant lots, using string trimmers, brush cutters and skid steers depending on the size of the job.

Weed & litter notices

The City of Albuquerque and the surrounding counties can issue notices requiring property owners to clear weeds and litter from their lots. If you have received one, read it carefully for the deadline and requirements, and call us — we can usually schedule a lot cleanup quickly.

We are not able to give legal advice about a notice or guarantee how an agency will respond, but we can do the work, send you before-and-after photos, and give you a receipt for your records.
